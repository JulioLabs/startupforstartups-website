A single page announcing what's coming, with a form for visitors to leave their email address.

## Set up the page

Click **Create**, then **Launch Page**. You set:

- a **Name**, the **Domain** and **Subdomain**;
- a **Heading** and **Subheading**;
- a **Theme**: Full Screen, Split Screen, Full Screen Countdown, Split Screen Countdown, Founder Letter, or Social Proof + Momentum. The countdown themes count down to a launch date you set.

## Collect email addresses

Under **Mailing List Options**, choose where sign-ups go:

- an existing [mailing list](/docs/services/mailing-lists/), or
- a **New Mailing List**, created for you.

If the list requires confirmation, each new subscriber gets a confirmation email, sent through your domain's email settings. **Set up email sending for the domain first** (see [Mailing lists](/docs/services/mailing-lists/#sending-email)), or subscribers won't receive it.
