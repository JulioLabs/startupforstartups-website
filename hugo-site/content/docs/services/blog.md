A blog on its own address, such as `blog.yourbusiness.com`, with a home page, a page for each post, category and tag pages, and an RSS feed.

## Set up the blog

Click **Create**, then **Blog**. You set:

- a **Name** (for you), a **Display Name** (shown as the blog's title) and a **Description**;
- the **Domain** and **Subdomain**;
- a **Theme**. There are seven: Basic, Minimal, Magazine, Elegant, Brutalist, Modern and Nightfall.

In the blog's settings you can also add a **Logo**, a **Banner Image**, and a **menu** of links, each with an optional dropdown of child links. Open **Styles** to change colors and fonts.

## Write a post

Open the blog and go to its posts, then create a new post. Each post has:

- a **Post Title** and the post itself;
- an **Excerpt**: a short summary shown in post lists;
- a **Category** (pick an existing one or type a new one) and **Tags** (separate them with commas);
- a **Meta Description (SEO)** for search results;
- a **Featured Image**, chosen from your assets.

## Publish, schedule or save a draft

- **Save Draft** keeps the post private.
- **Publish** puts it on your blog straight away.
- **Schedule** publishes it later: pick a date, a time (on the hour) and a time zone.

The posts list can be filtered by **Published**, **Drafts** and **Scheduled**. Tick several posts to publish, unpublish or delete them together.

## Show posts on your website

Add a [blog widget](/docs/widgets/blog-links-and-questionnaire-widgets/) to show your latest posts on your own site.

## Publish from other tools

Posts can also be created through the [API](/docs/reference/), for example to publish release notes automatically.
