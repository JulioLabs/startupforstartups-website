Services are pages that PresenceWave hosts for you, each on its own address. To start one, click **Create** in the sidebar and pick it under **Create Services**.

When you create a service, you choose:

- **Domain**: one of your own domains, or your [free presencewave.com address](/docs/domains/free-presencewave-address/).
- **Subdomain**: the first part of the address, such as `blog` for `blog.yourbusiness.com`. On your own domain, the form shows the [CNAME record](/docs/domains/subdomains-and-cname-records/) to add for it.

Mailing lists and questionnaires can also share a subdomain using a **path**, for example `news.yourbusiness.com/updates`. Every other service has a subdomain to itself.

You can move a service to another domain later: open it, change its **Domain**, and save. Links to the old address stop working, so you'll be asked to confirm.
