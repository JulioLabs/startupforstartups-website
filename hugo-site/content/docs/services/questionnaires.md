A questionnaire is a survey or form with one or more pages, at an address like `forms.yourbusiness.com/feedback`.

## Set up a questionnaire

Click **Create**, then **Questionnaire**. You set:

- a **Questionnaire Name** and **Description**;
- a **Header Title** and **Header Subtitle**, and a **Logo**;
- the **Domain**, **Subdomain** and **Path Prefix**. The path prefix is required, so one subdomain can hold several questionnaires, such as `/feedback` and `/onboarding`;
- a **Theme**: Basic Survey, Focus Mode, Structured or Compact;
- **Collect respondent email**: ask for an email address before the questions;
- a **Completion Message** shown at the end, or a **Redirect URL** to send people to another page instead.

**Changing the path prefix later** breaks links to the old address. You'll be warned first.

## Add pages and fields

A questionnaire needs at least one page. Give each page a **Page Title** and an optional **Page Description**, then add its fields. Each field can be required.

## Read the responses

Open the questionnaire to see **Total Responses**, how many are **Completed**, and each response with its status, progress and answers.

You can also send each response to another system with a [webhook](/docs/widgets/submissions/#get-submissions-somewhere-else).

## Put it on your website

Use a [questionnaire widget](/docs/widgets/blog-links-and-questionnaire-widgets/) to show it inside your own site.
