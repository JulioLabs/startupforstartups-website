A single page, such as `links.yourbusiness.com`, that collects your links in one place. It's the address to put in your social media bios.

## Set up the page

Click **Create**, then **Links Page**. You set:

- a **Name** (for you), a **Display Name** and an optional **Tagline**;
- the **Domain** and **Subdomain**;
- a **Company Logo** or picture;
- up to two buttons at the top: **Primary CTA** and **Secondary CTA**, each with text and an address;
- a **Theme**: Basic, Split Screen, Aurora Gradient, Creator Grid, Minimal Mono or Bento Blocks. Some themes can show your picture as a circle.

## Add links

Click **Add Link** for each link. A link has a **Title**, an optional **Description**, its **URL**, an **Icon** and **Button Text**. Drag links, or use the arrows, to change their order.

Click **Add Widget** to put a [contact form or form](/docs/widgets/forms/) on the page. Only forms on the same domain as the page are offered.

## Use it elsewhere

- Show the page inside your own website with a [links widget](/docs/widgets/blog-links-and-questionnaire-widgets/).
- Make a [QR code](/docs/services/short-links-and-qr-codes/) of its address for print.
