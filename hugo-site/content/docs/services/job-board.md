A careers page on its own address, such as `jobs.yourbusiness.com`, with a page for each job.

## Set up the job board

Click **Create**, then **Job Board**. You set a **Name**, a **Description**, the **Domain** and **Subdomain**, a **Logo** and a **Theme**.

The theme settings include:

- the **company name**, a headline and a subtitle;
- a **contact email**: the **Apply** button on each job opens an email to this address, with the job title in the subject. Until you set one, the button is shown but can't be clicked;
- filters visitors can use: by department, location and job type;
- an optional benefits section, mission statement and community message.

## Add a job

Open the job board and go to its jobs, then add a job. Each job has:

- a **Job Title** and description;
- **Skills**;
- a **Job Type** and **Remote Policy**;
- a **Department**;
- one or more locations (**City**, **State/Region**, **Country**).

Click **Save Draft** to keep a job private, or **Publish** to put it on your job board straight away. A job needs a description before it can be published. The jobs list can be filtered by **Published** and **Drafts**.
