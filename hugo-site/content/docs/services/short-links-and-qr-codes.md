## Short links

A URL shortener gives you short links on your own domain, such as `go.yourbusiness.com/spring-sale`, that send visitors on to any address.

### Set up the shortener

Click **Create**, then **URL Shortener**. Choose the **Domain** and a **Subdomain** (`go` is suggested). On your own domain, add the [CNAME record](/docs/domains/subdomains-and-cname-records/) the form shows.

### Create short links

Open the shortener's links, enter the **URL to shorten**, and optionally a **Custom code** for the last part of the link. Leave it empty and a code is made for you. Codes can use letters, numbers, `-` and `_`, up to 64 characters.

The list shows each link's **Clicks**: the number of visits sent on, not counting search engine crawlers and other bots. Use the copy button to copy a link.

## QR codes

Open **Tools** at the top right, then **QR codes**, to make a QR code for any address, for example your links page or a short link.

1. Give it a **Name** and enter the **URL**.
2. Optionally add a **Logo** in the middle, and change the **QR Color** and **Background Color**.
3. Choose the **Output Size**.
4. Check the **Live Preview**, then click **Download PNG** or **Download SVG**. SVG stays sharp at any size, so it's best for print.

Tip: point the QR code at a short link. You can then change where the short link goes without reprinting the code.
