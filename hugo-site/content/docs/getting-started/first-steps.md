Once your account is set up, here's a good order to work in.

## 1. Look at what was created

If you picked tools when you [created your account](/docs/getting-started/create-your-account/), they're already live on your free presencewave.com address. Open your domain in the sidebar to see them, and open each one to see its public address.

## 2. Make it yours

- **Blog**: write and publish your first post. See [Blog](/docs/services/blog/).
- **Links page**: replace the sample links with your own. See [Links page](/docs/services/links-page/).
- **Style**: every widget, and every service except short links, has a **Styles** page where you can change colors and fonts.

## 3. Connect your own domain

When you're ready for addresses like `blog.yourbusiness.com`:

1. [Add your domain and verify it](/docs/domains/add-a-custom-domain/) with a TXT record.
2. Add a [CNAME record](/docs/domains/subdomains-and-cname-records/) for each subdomain you'll use.
3. Move each service to your domain: open it, change its **Domain**, and save.

Your free address keeps working, so nothing breaks while you do this.

## 4. Add a widget to your website

Widgets such as contact forms, banners and surveys go on the website you already have. They work on **your own domain once it's verified**, so connect your domain first (step 3). Then:

1. Click **Create** and pick a widget.
2. Choose your domain, fill in the details and save.
3. Paste the embed code into your website.

See [Add a widget to your website](/docs/widgets/embed-a-widget/) for the details.
