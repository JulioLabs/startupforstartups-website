After you sign in, the dashboard opens. The sidebar on the left is organized **by domain**: everything you build belongs to one of your domains.

## The sidebar

At the top, **Dashboard** gives an overview of everything in your account.

Below it, each domain has its own section. Click the domain name to see all its services, or the arrow to expand or collapse it. Inside each domain:

| Group | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Primary** | Blog, Link Pages and Job Board |
| **Engage** | Mailing List and Widgets |
| **Insight** | Submissions (what visitors send through your widgets), Analytics, and the domain's Settings |

Where a domain has nothing of one kind yet, you'll see a "Create new…" link instead.

Your free presencewave.com address only appears in the sidebar while it has something on it, or when it's your only domain.

At the bottom of the sidebar:

- **Create**: start any service or widget. See [Services](/docs/services/) and [Widgets](/docs/widgets/).
- **Assets**: images and files you've uploaded, for use in posts, logos and pages.
- **Settings**: your domains (see [Domains](/docs/domains/)), team members and API tokens.
- **Billing**: your plan and domain slots (see [Plans and billing](/docs/billing/)).

## The header

At the top right:

- **Docs** and **API** open the reference documentation.
- **Tools** has extras that don't belong to a domain, such as **QR codes** (see [Short links and QR codes](/docs/services/short-links-and-qr-codes/)).
