Setting up takes three short steps and needs no credit card. You don't need to change any DNS settings to get started.

## 1. Sign in with your email

1. Go to [app.presencewave.com](https://app.presencewave.com/login).
2. Enter your email address and click **Send Secure Login Link**.
3. Open the email and click the link. It works once and expires after 15 minutes.

There's no password. The same link creates your workspace the first time and signs you in after that. If a link has expired, enter your email again to get a new one.

## 2. Choose your website address

Next you choose where your services will live. You have two options:

- **Use your own domain** (recommended). Type a domain you own, such as `yourbusiness.com`, and click **Continue with my domain**. You don't need to verify it yet: you can [connect it](/docs/domains/add-a-custom-domain/) whenever you're ready to go live.
- **Start with a free PresenceWave address.** Don't have a domain yet? Pick a name and click **Start with free address** to get an address like `yourbusiness.presencewave.com`.

Either way, you also get the [free presencewave.com address](/docs/domains/free-presencewave-address/). If you type your own domain, the free address is filled in to match it (for example `yourbusinesscom`); you can edit it before continuing. A green tick shows when the name is available.

## 3. Choose your tools

The **Choose your tools** page lists the services and widgets you can start with. These are ticked to begin with:

- Blog
- Job Board
- Link-in-Bio Page
- URL Shortener
- Launch Page
- Contact Form

Top Banner and Cookie Consent are also offered, but not ticked. Click a card to tick or untick it, then click **Set Up Selected Tools**.

Each tool you pick is created with sample content on your **free presencewave.com address**, so you can open it straight away. For example, the blog appears at `blog.yourbusiness.presencewave.com`. You can edit, move or delete any of them later.

Prefer to start from scratch? Click **Skip Setup**. You can add any of these later from **Create**.
