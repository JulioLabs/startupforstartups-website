Both widgets float in the bottom-right corner of the window, above the cookie banner if one is showing. Their widget code can go anywhere on the page. Answers are saved under [Submissions](/docs/widgets/submissions/).

## Survey

A survey asks one **Survey Question**, such as "Did you find what you were looking for?". The theme sets how visitors answer:

- **Default**: **Yes** or **No** buttons with thumbs up and down.
- **Emoji Rating (Dark)**: a 1–5 rating with faces.

Visitors can close the survey with the × button. After a visitor answers, the survey doesn't ask them again for the rest of their browser session.

## Feedback

A **Feedback** button stays in the corner. Clicking it opens a panel with:

- an **Overall rating** from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent);
- an optional comments box, up to 1,000 characters.

You set the panel's **Title**, **Subtitle**, **Submit Button Text** and **Thank You Message**.
