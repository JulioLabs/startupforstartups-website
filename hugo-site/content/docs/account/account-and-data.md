## Profile and company

Open **Settings** in the sidebar:

- **User Profile**: your **Full Name**, and the **Display Name** shown in the dashboard header instead of your email.
- **Blog Author Profile**: how you're credited on blog posts: a **Profile Image**, **Display Name**, **Bio** and **Link**. Each team member has their own.
- **Company Profile**: your **Company Name**. It must be unique on PresenceWave; the form tells you when the name is available.
- **Usage**: how many custom domains and how much storage you're using, against your limits. Your free presencewave.com address isn't counted.

## Activity history

The dashboard's activity feed shows recent changes in your account. Click **View All Activity** for the full **Activity History**: what was created, changed or deleted, when, and by whom. You can filter it by event type, by type of item and by date, and open an event for details such as the IP address and browser it came from.

## Export your data

You can download your data as CSV files:

- **Submissions**: on the Submissions page, **Export CSV** on each widget's tab. See [Submissions](/docs/widgets/submissions/).
- **Questionnaire responses**: **Export CSV** on the questionnaire.
- **Mailing list subscribers**: **Export CSV** on the subscriber list.

Submissions, questionnaire responses, subscribers, short links and assets can also be read through the [API](/docs/account/api-tokens/).

## Close your account

There's no button to close an account in the dashboard yet. To close yours or ask for your data to be deleted, email [sushrut@presencewave.com](mailto:sushrut@presencewave.com) from your account's email address.

When an account is deleted, you can ask for an export of your data for 30 days. After that, the data may be permanently deleted. See the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy/), section 5.

If you only want to stop paying, cancel your plan instead. See [Cancelling](/docs/billing/cancelling/). Your data is kept.

## Your visitors' data

What visitors send through your forms, surveys and questionnaires, and your mailing list subscribers, belongs to you. You can export it as above and delete submissions you don't need. The [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy/) explains how PresenceWave handles personal data.
