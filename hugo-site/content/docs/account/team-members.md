Everyone in your account can see and change everything in it. There are no roles or separate permissions.

## Invite someone

1. Open **Settings** in the sidebar and find **Security & Access**.
2. Enter one or more **Email Addresses**, separated by commas.
3. Click **Send Invitation** (**Send Invitations** for several).

Each person gets an email with a link to join. The link expires after 24 hours. Until then, the invitation is listed under pending invitations, with who sent it and when it expires. Click **Cancel** to withdraw it. Inviting the same address again sends a new link.

An email address that already has a PresenceWave account can't be invited: each person belongs to one account.

## Signing in

Team members sign in the same way you do, with a link sent to their email. There are no passwords. See [Create your account](/docs/getting-started/create-your-account/).

## Remove someone

Under **User Management** in **Settings**, click **Remove** next to the person and confirm. They lose access straight away.

- You can't remove yourself.
- An account always keeps at least one member.
