description: "Which addresses use a domain slot, and which are free."

Your plan includes a number of [domain slots](/docs/billing/domain-slots/), and each **domain you add** uses one. Here's what that means in practice.

| Address | Uses a slot? |
|---|---|
| A domain you add, such as `yourbusiness.com` | **Yes, one slot** |
| Its subdomains, such as `blog.yourbusiness.com`, `jobs.yourbusiness.com` or `go.yourbusiness.com` | No: they're part of `yourbusiness.com` |
| Your free address on `presencewave.com` | No, never |
| A second domain, such as `yourotherbusiness.com` | **Yes, one more slot** |

Some details:

- **A domain uses its slot as soon as you add it**, even before you've verified it.
- **Deleting a domain frees its slot straight away**, so you can add another one in its place.
- **There's no limit on subdomains.** Put your blog, job board, links page and newsletter on as many subdomains of one domain as you like.
- **The same domain can't be live on two accounts.** Only one account can verify a given domain.
