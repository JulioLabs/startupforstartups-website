---
title: "Your free presencewave.com address"
description: "The address every account gets, which never uses a slot and keeps working whatever your billing status."
weight: 40
---

Every account gets a free address on `presencewave.com`, such as `yourbusiness.presencewave.com`. You pick the name when you set up your account.

- It **never uses a [domain slot](/docs/billing/domain-slots/)**.
- It works straight away: there's nothing to verify and no DNS records to add.
- It **keeps working whatever your billing status**, even if your trial or plan has ended and your own domains have been switched off.

It's a good place to try services before you connect your own domain. Once your domain is connected, you can move a service to it: open the service in the dashboard and change its domain.

Some names are reserved and can't be used, such as `www`, `app`, `blog`, `docs` and `help`.
