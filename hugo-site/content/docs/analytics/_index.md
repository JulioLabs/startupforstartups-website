Each domain has its own analytics. Open **Analytics** in the domain's **Insight** section in the sidebar.

## What's counted

- **Pages on your own website** that have the [PresenceWave snippet](/docs/widgets/embed-a-widget/). Every page that loads it counts, even one without a widget.
- **Your PresenceWave services** on the domain: blog, job board, links page, launch page and questionnaires. These are counted automatically.
- **Short link clicks**, counted when a visitor is sent on.
- **Widget activity**: each widget shown on a page, and a visitor accepting the consent banner.

Visits from search engine crawlers, link previews, automated browsers and scripts aren't counted.

## Choose a time period

Pick **Today**, **Last Week** (7 days), **Last Month** (30 days) or **Last Year** (365 days). Each period includes today. Today's chart is by hour; the others are by day.

## What the page shows

- **Page Views** and **Total Events** for the period. Page views are page loads; total events also include short link clicks and widget activity.
- **Daily Traffic Trends** (**Today's Traffic** for today): page views over the period.
- **Properties Chart** and **Property Breakdown**: page views per service, for example your website, your blog and your links page.
- **Event Types**: how the events split between page views, short link clicks and each kind of widget event, such as *Widget rendered*.
- **Top Referrers**: the sites visitors came from.
- **Top Pages**: the most viewed addresses.

## Why the numbers differ from Google Analytics

The two tools count differently, so expect some difference:

- PresenceWave counts every page load. It doesn't count unique visitors or sessions.
- Only pages with the PresenceWave snippet, and PresenceWave's own services, are counted.
- Each tool filters bots in its own way, and browser privacy tools block some trackers but not others.

Submissions aren't part of analytics. See [Submissions](/docs/widgets/submissions/).
