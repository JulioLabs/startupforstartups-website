The **Billing** page shows your account's status next to **Your plan**. Some statuses also show a banner across the dashboard.

| Status | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Free trial** | Your trial is running. The Billing page shows the days left. | Choose a plan any time before it ends. You won't be charged before the trial ends. |
| **Trial ended** | The trial ended without a plan. Your domains keep working for 8 days, then your custom domains and their widgets stop. | Choose a plan. |
| **Active** | Your plan is running and renews on the date shown. | Nothing. |
| **Payment failed** | A payment couldn't be collected. Your domains keep working for now. | Click **Pay now**, then update your card in **Manage billing**. |
| **Ending** | You cancelled. The plan runs until the date shown. | Nothing, or reactivate it in **Manage billing**. |
| **Ended** | Your plan has ended. Your domains keep working for 30 days, then your custom domains and their widgets stop. | Choose a plan. |
| **Complimentary** | Your account has a fixed number of free slots, agreed with us. | [Contact us](mailto:sushrut@presencewave.com) if you need more. |

## Banners

| Banner | When it shows |
|---|---|
| "Your free trial ends in N days." | During the last 7 days of the trial. |
| "Your free trial has ended. Choose a plan to keep your sites online after (date)." | After the trial ended without a plan. |
| "Your last payment failed." | When a payment couldn't be collected. |
| "Your plan has ended. Choose a plan to keep your sites online after (date)." | After your plan ended. |
| "Your custom domains and widgets are offline." | After the 8 or 30 days, once your custom domains have been switched off. |
| "You have N domains and M slots. Remove a domain or add a slot." | When you have more domains than slots. See [Domain slots](/docs/billing/domain-slots/#more-domains-than-slots). |
