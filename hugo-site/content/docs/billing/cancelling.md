You can cancel at any time. Cancelling takes effect at the **end of your current billing period**: you keep everything you've paid for until then.

## How to cancel

1. Open **Billing** in the dashboard and click **Manage billing**.
2. In our billing provider's portal, cancel your subscription.

Your plan then shows **Ending**, with the date it ends. Changed your mind? Reactivate it in **Manage billing** before that date.

## When your plan ends

When the plan ends, either because you cancelled it or because a payment couldn't be collected:

1. You can't add new domains. Your account has no domain slots until you choose a plan again.
2. Everything keeps working for **30 more days**. A banner in the dashboard shows the exact date.
3. After that, your custom domains and the widgets on them stop being served. Visitors to those addresses see a "not found" page, and widgets embedded on your own website stop showing.
4. Your free `presencewave.com` address keeps working.

Your account and everything in it are kept: services, posts, subscribers, form responses and settings. A plan ending is not the same as closing your account.

## Coming back

Choose a plan on the **Billing** page at any time. Your custom domains and widgets come back online as soon as the payment goes through.

## Refunds

Payments are not refunded when you cancel. The details are in our [Cancellation and Refund Policy](/legal/refund-cancellation/).
