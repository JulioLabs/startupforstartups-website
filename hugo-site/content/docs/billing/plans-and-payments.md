PresenceWave bills your **account**, not each domain. Your account has one plan, and each plan includes a number of [domain slots](/docs/billing/domain-slots/). Every plan includes every feature; plans differ in how many domains they include and whether you pay monthly or yearly.

The current plans and prices are on the [pricing page](/pricing/) and on the **Billing** page in the dashboard.

## Choosing a plan

1. Open **Billing** in the dashboard.
2. Under **Choose a plan**, pick a plan and click **Choose**.
3. You're taken to our billing provider's secure checkout page to pay. We never see or store your card details.
4. When you're done, you come back to the dashboard and your plan shows as **Active**.

A few things to know:

- **During the trial**, your first payment is taken when the trial ends, so choosing early costs nothing extra.
- **If you already have more domains than the plan includes**, checkout adds extra slots for them, so you don't go over your limit by paying.
- **After the trial ended, or after a plan ended**, choosing a plan brings any custom domains and widgets that were switched off back online.

## Currency

Prices are in US dollars, or in Indian rupees for customers in India. The Billing page shows prices in your local currency and lets you switch before you choose your first plan. Cards issued in India can only pay in INR.

Your account stays in the currency of your first plan: later plan changes and extra slots are billed in the same currency.

## Renewals and invoices

Plans renew automatically at the end of each billing period, monthly or yearly. The Billing page shows the renewal date.

**Manage billing** on the Billing page opens our billing provider's secure portal, where you can:

- see and download your invoices;
- update your card or add a payment method;
- cancel your plan (see [Cancelling your plan](/docs/billing/cancelling/)).

## Changing your plan

While your plan is active, the Billing page lists the other plans under **Change plan**. Click **Switch** next to the one you want. Your domain slots move to the new plan, and the next page shows what you pay now, or the credit you get for the rest of your current term, before you confirm.

## When a payment fails

If a renewal payment fails, your plan shows **Payment failed** and a banner appears across the dashboard. Your domains and widgets keep working while you fix it:

1. Click **Pay now** on the Billing page to pay the open invoice.
2. Open **Manage billing** and update your card, so the next renewal goes through.

If the payment still can't be collected, the plan ends, and the rules in [Cancelling your plan](/docs/billing/cancelling/#when-your-plan-ends) apply.
