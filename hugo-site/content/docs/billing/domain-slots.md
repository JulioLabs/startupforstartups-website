A **domain slot** lets you connect one domain of your own, such as `yourbusiness.com`. Your plan includes some slots, and you can add extra ones at any time. The Billing page shows how many you have and how many are in use, for example "2 of 3 used".

What uses a slot, and what doesn't, is explained in [What counts as a domain](/docs/domains/what-counts-as-a-domain/). In short: each domain you add uses one slot, its subdomains (`blog.yourbusiness.com`, `go.yourbusiness.com`) don't, and your free `presencewave.com` address never does.

## Adding a slot

You can add a slot while your plan is active and your account has a card on file.

1. Open **Billing** in the dashboard and click **Add a slot**.
2. The dialog shows exactly what you'll pay before you confirm.
3. Click **Add slot**. The slot works straight away, and you can add another domain.

**Every new slot is free for its first 30 days.** After that it's billed with your plan, at the slot price shown in the dialog. How the free month works depends on when your plan renews:

- **Your plan renews within the next 30 days** (always the case for monthly plans): nothing is charged now. If the renewal falls inside the free month, your next bill includes the slot and a credit for the rest of the free month, so the first 30 days stay free.
- **Your plan renews more than 30 days from now** (yearly plans): you pay now only for the time between the end of the free month and your renewal. From the renewal on, the slot is billed with your plan.

Amounts are rounded down to whole dollars or rupees, and very small amounts are waived.

## Removing a slot

You can remove an **extra** slot (not one included in your plan) when it isn't in use: delete a domain first if all your slots are taken.

1. Open **Billing** and click **Remove a slot**.
2. The slot is removed at your next renewal, and you're not billed for it after that. You can keep using it until then. Nothing is refunded.

Changed your mind before the renewal? Click **Keep my slots** on the Billing page.

## Swapping one domain for another

Deleting a domain frees its slot for another one straight away, with no change to your bill. Deleting a domain also deletes everything on it, so if you want to keep a service or widget, move it to the other domain first: open it in the dashboard and change its domain. Most services and widgets can be moved this way. Moving changes its address, so update any links or embed codes that point to the old one.

## More domains than slots

You can end up with more domains than slots, for example after switching to a plan with fewer domains. When that happens:

- all your existing domains **keep working**;
- you can't add another domain;
- a banner in the dashboard says, for example, "You have 3 domains and 2 slots. Remove a domain or add a slot."

Some accounts, such as agencies with an agreement with us, show **Complimentary** on the Billing page with a fixed number of slots. To change the number of slots, [contact us](mailto:sushrut@presencewave.com).
