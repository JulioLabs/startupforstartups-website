Every new account starts with a **30-day free trial**. You don't need a card to start it, and nothing is charged during it.

## What's included

- Every PresenceWave feature: all services, all widgets, analytics and the API.
- **One domain slot**, so you can connect one domain of your own, such as `yourbusiness.com`. See [What counts as a domain](/docs/domains/what-counts-as-a-domain/).
- Your free `presencewave.com` address, which never uses a slot.

The Billing page in the dashboard shows how many days are left and the date the trial ends. From 7 days before the end, a banner across the dashboard reminds you.

## Choosing a plan during the trial

You can choose a plan at any time from the **Billing** page. Choosing early costs nothing extra: your first payment is taken when the trial would have ended, not on the day you choose. See [Choosing a plan and paying](/docs/billing/plans-and-payments/).

## When the trial ends without a plan

1. You can't add new domains. Your account has no domain slots until you choose a plan.
2. Everything keeps working for **8 more days**. The banner in the dashboard shows the exact date.
3. After that, your custom domains and the widgets on them stop being served. Visitors to those addresses see a "not found" page, and widgets embedded on your own website stop showing.
4. Your free `presencewave.com` address keeps working, and nothing is deleted: your services, posts, subscribers and settings all stay in your account.

As soon as you choose a plan, your custom domains and widgets come back online.
