## Themes

Most services and widgets have several themes. Choose one in the service's or widget's form when you create or edit it: the preview images show what each theme looks like. Some themes also have their own settings, shown under the theme picker. The job board's contact email is one example.

You can switch theme at any time. Style changes you've made are kept.

## The style editor

To open the style editor:

- **Services:** open the service and click **Edit Styles** (or **Customize Styles** for mailing lists).
- **Widgets:** open **Widgets** under the domain and click the **Customize Styles** button next to the widget, or **Edit Styles** in the widget's form.

The editor shows the settings on the left and a live preview of your page or widget on the right. Changes save themselves a moment after you make them.

### Simple and advanced settings

The editor opens with the essential settings: the palette colors and the font. Click **Enable All Settings** for everything else, grouped in tabs, and use **Search settings** to find one. **Disable Advanced Mode** goes back to the short list.

Color settings offer **Quick Colors** taken from the theme, as well as a color picker.

### Other buttons

- **Use domain palette** copies your domain's [brand palette](/docs/styling/brand-palette/) into this component. It's disabled until the domain has a palette.
- **Import** copies the palette colors and fonts from another of your services or widgets, so they match.
- **Reset** puts back the theme's defaults. All your style changes to this component are lost, and this can't be undone.
- **Docs** opens this component's page in the [style reference](https://static.presencewave.com/docs/styles/styles.html), which lists every style setting and its default.

## Things to know

- Style changes show on your live pages straight away.
- Fonts come from Google Fonts.
