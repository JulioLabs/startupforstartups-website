<div class="sc-chapter-head">
<span class="sc-step">01</span>
<div><div class="rp-eyebrow">Build</div>
<h2 class="rp-h2">Add the questions you need, in the order you want.</h2></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-split">
<div class="sc-copy">
<h3>Start empty, or from a preset</h3>
<p>Give the form a name and choose the website it goes on. Then start from one of the ready-made forms (free trial, demo request, contact sales, support) or from an empty form, as Havenly did here.</p>
<ul class="sc-list">
<li><i class="fas fa-check"></i> Seven field types, from a one-line answer to a group of checkboxes</li>
<li><i class="fas fa-check"></i> Make any field required, and set placeholder text and a maximum length</li>
<li><i class="fas fa-check"></i> Give dropdowns, radio buttons and checkbox groups their own options</li>
<li><i class="fas fa-check"></i> Move fields up and down until the order feels right</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="sc-frame"><img src="/assets/images/showcase/form-builder/03-field-with-options.webp" alt="Adding a dropdown field with three options in the form builder" loading="lazy" width="600" height="700"></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-copy sc-copy-wide">
<h3>Your words, not ours</h3>
<p>Write the form's heading, the text on its button and the thank-you message people see after sending. "Get in touch" and "Send message" fit a contact form; "Book a deep clean" and "Check availability" fit a booking form.</p>
</div>
