<!-- 1. New patients -->
<section class="sc-chapter">
<div class="container">
<div class="sc-chapter-head">
<span class="sc-step">01</span>
<div><div class="rp-eyebrow">Welcome new patients</div>
<h2 class="rp-h2">From "are you taking new patients?" to a booked first visit.</h2></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-split">
<div class="sc-copy">
<h3>A request form that asks only what the front desk needs</h3>
<p>Brightwater's new-patient form asks for a name and phone number, the type of visit, who's coming and the best time, plus the patient's dental plan if they have one. Nothing about symptoms or history: those wait for the chair, and a note beside the form says so. The front desk calls back within a day, and every request lands in the dashboard and the office inbox at once.</p>
<ul class="sc-list">
<li><i class="fas fa-check"></i> Text, email and dropdown fields, styled to the site</li>
<li><i class="fas fa-check"></i> Ask for contact details and times, not medical details</li>
<li><i class="fas fa-check"></i> A short contact form in the footer for everything else</li>
<li><i class="fas fa-check"></i> Requests can go straight to your scheduling software with webhooks</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="sc-frame"><img src="/assets/images/showcase/dental-clinics/02-widget-quote-form.webp" alt="Brightwater's new-patient request form with visit type, who's coming and best time" loading="lazy"></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-split sc-split-reverse">
<div class="sc-copy">
<h3>An offer for patients without insurance</h3>
<p>A banner across the top of every page offers a $99 new-patient exam, cleaning and X-rays for patients without insurance. When the offer changes, Brightwater updates it from the dashboard, with no website update. A one-question poll asks whether visitors would like a text when their next cleaning is due, and a feedback tab catches anyone who couldn't find the hours or the insurance list.</p>

<div class="sc-copy">

<ul class="sc-list">

<li><i class="fas fa-check"></i> About the experience, not the patient's health</li>
