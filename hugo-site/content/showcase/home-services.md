<h3>A request form that sorts the urgent from the routine</h3>
<p>When the water heater is leaking, people book whoever answers first. Every request lands in the dashboard and the office inbox at once.</p>
<ul class="sc-list">
<li><i class="fas fa-check"></i> Text, dropdown and multiple-choice fields</li>
<li><i class="fas fa-check"></i> Styled to the site — no "embedded form" look</li>
<li><i class="fas fa-check"></i> A short contact form in the footer for questions that can wait</li>
<li><i class="fas fa-check"></i> Requests can go straight to your scheduling or CRM software with webhooks</li>
</ul>
