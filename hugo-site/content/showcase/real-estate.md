<h3>A market analysis request that sellers actually fill in</h3>
<p>Homeowners try the quick estimate on Marlow's home page, then ask for the real number. Dana calls back within a day to book the walk-through, and every request lands in the dashboard and the team inbox at once.</p>
<ul class="sc-list">
<li><i class="fas fa-check"></i> Text, email and dropdown fields, styled to the site</li>
<li><i class="fas fa-check"></i> The seller's timeline, so the hottest leads get called first</li>
<li><i class="fas fa-check"></i> A short contact form in the footer for buyers' questions</li>
<li><i class="fas fa-check"></i> Requests can go straight to your CRM with webhooks</li>
</ul>
