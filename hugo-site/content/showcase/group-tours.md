<!-- 1. Turn browsers into reservations -->
<section class="sc-chapter">
<div class="container">
<div class="sc-chapter-head">
<span class="sc-step">01</span>
<div><div class="rp-eyebrow">Turn browsers into travelers</div>
<h2 class="rp-h2">Turn a look at the dates into a held seat.</h2></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-split">
<div class="sc-copy">
<h3>A reservation form that asks about the trip</h3>
<p>Group trips sell on dates and seats. Wayfarer's reserve-a-seat form asks which trip, which month, how many travelers and what kind of room, sits right under the departure calendar, and sends every request to the dashboard and the owner's inbox, so the seat can be held the same day.</p>
<ul class="sc-list">
<li><i class="fas fa-check"></i> Text, email, dropdown and multiple-choice fields</li>
<li><i class="fas fa-check"></i> Styled to the site — no "embedded form" look</li>
<li><i class="fas fa-check"></i> A short contact form in the footer for quick questions</li>
<li><i class="fas fa-check"></i> Requests can go straight to your booking system with webhooks</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="sc-frame"><img src="/assets/images/showcase/group-tours/02-widget-quote-form.webp" alt="Wayfarer's reserve-a-seat form with trip, month, travelers and room" loading="lazy"></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-split sc-split-reverse">
<div class="sc-copy">
<h3>Give people a reason to book now</h3>
<p>The early booking offer, $250 off any 2027 trip booked before December 31, runs as a banner across the top of every page. Wayfarer changes the text or turns it off from the dashboard, with no website update. A one-question poll asks visitors whether they found a trip that suits them, and a feedback tab lets them say what's missing before they leave.</p>
