<h2 class="rp-h2">Turn a visit into a price request.</h2></div>

<div class="sc-copy">
<h3>A quote form that asks about the lawn</h3>
<p>Lawn care prices depend on the details: the ZIP code, the size of the lawn, mowing or treatments, how often. Greenstripe's quote form asks exactly that, sits inside its own pricing section, and sends every request to the dashboard and the owner's inbox.</p>
<ul class="sc-list">
<li><i class="fas fa-check"></i> Text, email, dropdown and multiple-choice fields</li>
<li><i class="fas fa-check"></i> Styled to the site — no "embedded form" look</li>
<li><i class="fas fa-check"></i> A short contact form in the footer for quick questions</li>
<li><i class="fas fa-check"></i> Submissions can go straight to a CRM with webhooks</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="sc-copy">
