<!-- FAQ -->
<section class="pr-faq" id="faq">
<div class="container">

<div class="rp-eyebrow">Questions</div>
<h2 class="rp-h2">Good to know.</h2>

<div class="pr-faq-list">

<details class="pr-faq-item">
<summary>Do I need a card to start?</summary>
<p>No. Your 30-day trial starts when you sign up. Choose a plan whenever you're ready; nothing is charged before the trial ends.</p>
</details>

<details class="pr-faq-item">
<summary>What happens when my trial ends?</summary>
<p>Choose a plan to keep going. If you don't, everything keeps working for 8 more days, so you have time to decide. After that, your custom domains and the widgets for those domains stop being served until you choose a plan; your free <code>presencewave.com</code> address keeps working.</p>
</details>

<details class="pr-faq-item">
<summary>How many team members can I have?</summary>
<p>As many as you like. Every plan includes unlimited team members at no extra cost, so you can invite your whole team, your agency or your freelancers.</p>
</details>

<details class="pr-faq-item">
<summary>Will new features cost extra?</summary>
<p>No. Every feature we release is switched on for all existing customers by default, on the plan you already have.</p>
</details>

<details class="pr-faq-item">
<summary>What counts as a domain?</summary>
<p>Each of your own domains, like <code>yourbusiness.com</code>, with all its subdomains (<code>blog.</code>, <code>jobs.</code>, <code>links.</code> and so on). Your free <code>presencewave.com</code> address is never counted.</p>
</details>

<details class="pr-faq-item">
<summary>How does adding a domain work?</summary>
<p>Add one from the Billing page at any time, for the same price per domain as your plan. Every new domain's first 30 days are free; after that it renews with the rest of your plan.</p>
</details>

<details class="pr-faq-item">
<summary>Can I cancel any time?</summary>
<p>Yes. Your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for, then stops. See our <a href="/legal/refund-cancellation/">cancellation and refund policy</a>.</p>
</details>

<details class="pr-faq-item">
<summary>Is there a discount for paying yearly?</summary>
<p>Yes: $199 a year per domain instead of $348, a saving of 43%.</p>
</details>

<details class="pr-faq-item">
<summary>Which payment methods do you accept?</summary>
<p>Major credit and debit cards, processed securely by our billing provider. We never see your card details.</p>
</details>
