<div class="section-header">
<h2 class="section-title">Lead Generation Forms FAQ</h2>
<p class="section-description">
Common questions about building high-converting lead capture forms
</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-grid">
<div class="faq-item">
<h3>How quickly can I create a lead generation form?</h3>
<p>You can create a professional lead generation form in under 5 minutes. Start from a ready-made preset (free trial sign-up, demo request, contact sales, support and more) or an empty form, add fields from seven types, mark them required and put them in the order you want, without any coding.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-item">
<h3>Do lead forms work on all websites and platforms?</h3>
<p>Yes. Our forms work on any website that lets you add custom code, including WordPress, Shopify themes, static sites, custom web apps and most landing page builders. Simply copy and paste the embed code - no plugins required.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-item">
<h3>What kinds of questions can my form ask?</h3>
<p>Forms have seven field types: short text, email, long text, dropdown, radio buttons, a single checkbox and a group of checkboxes. Dropdowns, radio buttons and checkbox groups get their own options, so you can build contact, booking, sign-up and feedback forms with the same builder.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-item">
<h3>How do I receive leads from my forms?</h3>
<p>Get instant email notifications for every form submission, view all leads in your dashboard, and export data to CSV. We also support webhooks and API integration to connect with your favorite tools like email marketing platforms and CRMs.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-item">
<h3>Are the forms mobile-friendly and responsive?</h3>
<p>Yes, all forms are automatically optimized for mobile devices and tablets. They adapt perfectly to any screen size while maintaining functionality and visual appeal, ensuring you never lose leads due to poor mobile experience.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-item">
<h3>Can I customize the design of my lead forms?</h3>
<p>Yes. In the style editor you set your brand colour, backgrounds and text colours once, pick any Google Font, and fine-tune labels, inputs, dropdowns, buttons and the success message. The live preview updates as you go, and the form on your site picks up the changes on its next page load.</p>
</div>
