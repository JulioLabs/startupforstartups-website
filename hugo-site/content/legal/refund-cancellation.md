# Cancellation and Refund Policy

**Effective Date:** [to be set on publication]

This policy applies to paid plans of PresenceWave (the "Service"), operated by Julio Labs (OPC) Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us"). It is part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

## 1. Free trial

Every new account starts with a 30-day free trial. No payment details are needed to start it, and nothing is charged during it. If you choose a plan during the trial, your first payment is taken when the trial ends. If the trial ends without a plan, everything keeps working for 8 more days. After that, your custom domains and the widgets for those domains stop being served until you choose a plan; your free presencewave.com address keeps working.

## 2. Plans and billing

Plans are billed in advance, monthly or yearly, in US dollars, and renew automatically at the end of each billing period until cancelled. Each plan includes a number of domains. Extra domain slots can be added at any time; the first 30 days of each new slot are free, and any amount for the rest of the current period is shown before you confirm.

Payments are processed by our billing provider, Chargebee, through Razorpay. We never see or store your card details.

## 3. Cancelling

You can cancel at any time from **Billing → Manage billing** in your dashboard. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period: your plan, domains and services keep working until then, and you are not charged again. You can reactivate before the period ends. After the plan ends, everything keeps working for 30 more days. After that, your custom domains and the widgets for those domains stop being served until you choose a plan again; your free presencewave.com address keeps working.

Removing a domain slot also takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

## 4. Refunds

All payments are non-refundable, including for partially used billing periods, unused domain slots and plans cancelled before the end of the period, unless a refund is required by applicable law.

If you were charged in error (for example, charged twice for the same period), contact us within 30 days and we will correct it.

## 5. Failed payments

If a payment fails, we and our billing provider will notify you and retry it. You can pay an outstanding invoice from **Billing → Pay now**. If it remains unpaid, your plan may be cancelled at the end of the retry period, and the 30 days described in section 3 then apply.
